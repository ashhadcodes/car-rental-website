Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you navigate driver requirements, policies, and reservations.

To rent a vehicle, you need a valid government-issued driver's license (held for at least 1 year), to be at least 21 years of age, and a valid payment card in the driver's name. International renters are welcome with a valid foreign driver's license and passport.
Yes! All CarGo reservations include 100% Free Cancellation with a full instant refund if cancelled up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time. Modifications can also be made free of charge.
Standard state-mandated third-party liability coverage is included with every rental. For total peace of mind, you can add our Comprehensive Zero-Deductible protection plan for $20/day, which covers collision, tire, glass, and scratch damage with $0 customer liability.
Yes, one-way rentals are supported across all our major hubs (Airport Terminals, Downtown Metro, Financial District, and Resort Marina). Simply choose your desired drop-off hub when searching or booking.
The minimum rental period is 24 hours (1 day). We also offer discounted extended rates for weekly and monthly rentals.
Standard Economy, Sedans, and SUVs include Unlimited Mileage throughout the country. Exotic high-performance sports cars (such as the Porsche 911) include a generous allowance of 200 miles per day, with additional miles available at a fair flat rate.
